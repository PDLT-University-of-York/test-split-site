# Mentimeter

!!! Summary
    [Mentimeter](https://www.mentimeter.com/) is our University-supported web-based tool for adding polling and other interaction to presentations or sessions.

## Overview

Participants respond online to the variety of questions available through a mobile device or laptop by scanning a QR code or entering a ‘session code’ at [http://www.menti.com](http://www.menti.com). You can then access or share these responses during and after the presentation. No special software is required.

[Mentimeter’s own help pages](https://help.mentimeter.com/en/) are comprehensive and well categorised, and individual guides contain step-by step instructions with screenshots and/or video walkthroughs.  

These pages provide information on Mentimeter at the University of York with case studies from staff making use of the tool to support active and inclusive learning, opportunities to access training and resources and join communication channels, and guidance on how to use it at the University.

## Accessibility

Mentimeter have published the following [accessibility statement](https://www.mentimeter.com/accessibility) offering an overview of the challenges for users with different disabilities and incorporating an analysis of how the tool meets the standards of the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines. They have also provided the following guidance on how to [present with accessibility and inclusion in mind](https://help.mentimeter.com/en/articles/4280075-present-with-accessibility-and-inclusion-in-mind).  Please contact us if you would like to discuss the accessibility of Mentimeter further.
