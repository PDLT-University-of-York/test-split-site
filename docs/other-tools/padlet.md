# Padlet

!!! warning

    Due to unexpected licensing changes leading to rising costs, the University of York will no longer be able to use Padlet, effective from the start of the next academic year (2025-26).

    There will be no access to the platform past that point, either to create new Padlet boards, or to access/view/update existing ones in a “read only” mode or otherwise.

    Padlet will no longer be available at the University of York after **September 30th, 2025**.

We will update this page shortly with further details of:

- how to export your Padlet content before access is lost.
- alternative platforms that are available to replace Padlet.

For more details, please see our [blog post: Padlet access to be retired]( https://elearningyork.wpcomstaging.com/2025/09/04/system-update-padlet-access-to-be-retired/).
