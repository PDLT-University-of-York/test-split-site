# Using University templates and changing the style of your slides

!!! Summary
     You can change the style of your slides in Mentimeter and use University themes which have been designed for high contrast using the University brand colours.

If you would like to use a University of York style for your Mentimeter slides you can do so by selecting ‘Themes’ from the 'design' options.

[decorative](images/menti_themes.png)

Then select one of the University of York themes. These have been designed for high contrast using the University brand colour scheme as of 2023.

[decorative](images/menti_uni_themes.png)

You can also select from other [Mentimeter theme options](https://help.mentimeter.com/en/articles/410482-change-the-theme-of-your-presentation) or [create your own theme](https://help.mentimeter.com/en/articles/410484-create-your-own-themes).  Themes also allow you to change the font used in your presentations.
