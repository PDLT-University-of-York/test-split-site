# Artificial Intelligence tools

!!! Summary

    Tools that use Generative AI (GenAI) to create, analyse or manipulate content based on user-inputted prompts or existing data.


## Top tips for using AI tools effectively

<div markdown class="grid">
<div markdown>
- **Do you need AI?**: consider the environmental impact related to GenAI and whether the specific AI tool is needed and appropriate for your intended use case.
- **A starting point**: output as ideas generation or a quick start for your own content development rather than a finished product.
- **Effective input**: specific prompts and input content can help generate appropriate output. See our  tool guides for situation-specific tips or the Skills Guides for [general tips on writing good prompts](https://subjectguides.york.ac.uk/digital-creativity/artificial-intelligence#s-lg-box-wrapper-19475682). 
- **Check everything**: make sure that output is accurate and appropriate for your intended use and adapt as needed.
