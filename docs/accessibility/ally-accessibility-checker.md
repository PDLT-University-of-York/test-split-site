# Ally

!!! Summary
    Ally is a tool that works seamlessly on the Learn VLE to provide tips and step-by-step guidance to help staff improve their content accessibility. In addition to providing staff with an accessibility report for their VLE site, Ally automatically creates alternative versions of your files. This allows students to choose the type of file they want to download that best suits their needs. While you're in the process of improving files, students can still access alternative copies.
