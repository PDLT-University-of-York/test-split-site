# Screenshots

!!! Summary
    Screenshots or photographs of your issue and/or any error messages you receive is helps us to understand and troubleshoot your issue.

## Taking Screenshots

!!! Success

    Make sure that screenshots include your entire screen, and not just a part of it.

How you take screenshots varies depending on what device you're using (eg. a Windows PC differs from an iPhone). We've included links below for screenshotting on most common devices and operating systems.

- [Windows Computer](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/use-snipping-tool-to-capture-screenshots-00246869-1843-655f-f220-97299b865f6b)
- [Apple Computer (aka Mac/MacBook)](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/HT201361#:~:text=To%20take%20a%20screenshot%2C%20press,be%20saved%20to%20your%20desktop.)
- [Chromebook](https://support.google.com/chromebook/answer/10474268?hl=en-GB)
- [Linux Computer](https://www.wikihow.com/Take-a-Screenshot-in-Linux)
- [iPhone](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/HT200289)
- [iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/guide/ipad/ipad08a40f3b/ipados)
- [Android Phones and Tablets](https://support.google.com/android/answer/9075928?hl=en-GB).

## Attaching Files to an Email
Once you have your photographs and/or screenshots, attach them to the email that you send to us.
[Guidance on attaching files in GMail](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/6584?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop).
