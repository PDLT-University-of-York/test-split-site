# CMALT: professional development

!!! Summary

    We can support you to develop, reflect on and gain accreditation for your learning technology practice through Certified Membership of the Association for Learning Technology (CMALT).

## CMALT Accreditation Framework

CMALT is a portfolio-based peer-reviewed accreditation, organised by the [Association for Learning Technology](https://www.alt.ac.uk/certified-membership). It's open to anyone who engages with learning technology - it's not just for tech teams.

Colleagues have undertaken writing a CMALT portfolio to:

- critically reflect on their practice.
- allocate time to learning about a new aspect of technology-enhanced learning.
- build up evidence of professional development for other accreditation such as [Higher Education Academy (HEA) fellowships](https://www.york.ac.uk/staff/teaching/develop/advance-he-fellowship/).
