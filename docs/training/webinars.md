# Webinars

!!! Summary

    Sessions to share knowledge and explore best practice in implementing digital education tools.


## Digital Education Team webinars

We run regular webinars where colleagues from across the university share their experience with digital education tools, followed by discussion and reflection.

Visit our [webinars webpage](https://elearningyork.wpcomstaging.com/professional-development-opportunities/lunchtime-webinar-series/) for details of upcoming sessions and to access previous session slides and recordings. We have also included some case studies from previous webinars in the relevant guides.


## Future Teacher webinars

Our Digital Education Adviser Lilian Joy is also a co-host of the **Future Teacher Talks** webinar series, which covers learning and teaching with technology more broadly. The interactive sessions have a strong emphasis on inclusivity, open access and sharing practice. 

Visit the [Future Teacher Talks webpage](https://xot.futureteacher.eu/play.php?template_id=4#home) for details of upcoming sessions and to access previous session recordings.
