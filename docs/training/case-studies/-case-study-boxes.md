# Case study: Supporting transition and skills development through technology-enabled dialogue and feedback
lls-dantec

!!! case-study "Case study: Supporting transition and skills development through technology-enabled dialogue and feedback"

    Cathy Dantec explains how she uses dialogic feedback interactions between individual students and intructors (face-to-face and via Google Docs) to help first year students adjust to the transition to university study.

    The overall aims were to:

    - Improve students’ transition to university study, promoting self-regulation.
    - Provide a framework for investigating effective feedback, from the perspective of both tutors and students.
    - Ensure consistent and high quality teaching without increasing staff hours.
    - Improve student’s transferable skills, employability and general digital literacy.

    See the [full case study for an in-depth discussion of the intervention and outcomes](../training/case-studies/lls-dantec.md).
