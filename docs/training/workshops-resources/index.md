## Available sessions

You can browse the available sessions and sign up using the embedded google form below. 

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   **Getting started with Ultra**

    ---

    For any colleagues who have joined the University in the last year, this session will provide an essential introduction to the Blackboard Learn Ultra VLE.

</div>

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   **Prepare your VLE sites for teaching - Guided walkthrough**

    ---

    Whether you have a site with content rolled over from previous years or a new site from template, you will need to prepare your site for teaching. This session will guide you through this process, helping you to meet all of the essential VLE site design principles.

</div>

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   **Preparing your sites for teaching - Power Hour**

    ---
    
    Ring-fenced and supported time to prepare your site(s):

     - 5 minutes sharing what you will do during the hour.
     - 50 minutes working on your site. If you have questions while you are working on your site, there is a breakout room you can join to ask questions and get help from your host.
     - 5 minutes sharing what you’ve achieved and what more you have to do.

</div>

<div class="grid cards" markdown>
-   **Getting started with Mentimeter**

    ---
    
    This session will provide you with a 'student experience' of Mentimeter, showing you a wide range of question and interaction types that can be used to support teaching.  We will then go 'under the hood' to show you how to create and deliver your own Mentimeter presentations.

</div>

<div class="grid cards" markdown>
-   **Getting started with Padlet**

    ---
    
    Padlet is a flexible web-based tool for collaborative and individual project work and discussion. This session will introduce you to the tool and show you how to set up and deploy padlet boards for teaching and learning.

</div>

<div class="grid cards" markdown>
-   **Getting started with VLE Tests and quizzes**

    ---
    
    Learn how to set up and deploy quizzes and tests within your VLE sites to support learning, teaching and assessment.

</div>

<div class="grid cards" markdown>
-   **Ultra Assignment marking**

    ---
    
    Ultra assignment can be used for individual and group submissions of varying types and formats.  This session provides guidance on marking and feedback for Ultra assignment.

</div>
