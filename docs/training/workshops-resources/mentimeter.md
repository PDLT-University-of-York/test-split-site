# Mentimeter training and resources

!!! Summary
    Take part in hands-on training activities and access resources and on-demand courses to develop your skills in using Mentimeter.

We do not currently run a dedicated Mentimeter workshop, however there are many resources that you can access at any time.

## Mentimeter's own training & resources

Mentimeter host regular [live webinars](https://www.mentimeter.com/webinars) with [recordings](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLsFGssspaTWeRRGQVF2uhQO4rcAByS5-t) available on demand. These include regular ‘Mentimeter for Educators’ sessions as well as more general webinars.

The ‘[Mentimeter academy](https://academy.mentimeter.com/)’ also offers a range of on-demand courses for those with a range of existing knowledge and experience, from complete beginners to advanced users.

The latest offerings include:

- [Neuroscience Based Learning: Strategies for making your content stick](https://academy.mentimeter.com/courses/neuroscience-based-learning)
- [Inclusive teaching: Strategies for engaging all students](https://academy.mentimeter.com/courses/inclusive-teaching-strategies-for-engaging-all-students)

You can access these short courses at your own pace, engage with example questions and presentations, and copy the presentations used into your own Mentimeter account to adapt and use with your own students.
