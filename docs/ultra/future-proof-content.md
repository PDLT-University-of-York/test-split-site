# Create future proofing content

!!! Summary

    It is important to make sure that your students have access to the most-up-to-date information, especially after site rollover each year. To future-proof your course content in Ultra site, focus on producing quality content from the start. It may take a bit longer up front, but it will pay off in the long run. This guide provides simple strategies you can take to make your site rollover next year easier and less cognitively demanding. 


## Quick Guidance
<!-- Summary/key considerations -->

### **What does future proofing your content mean?**

Future-proofing means preparing your course content not only for this year, but also for future site rollover. It means creating content that is versatile, flexible enough to be updated for years to come. If you start out by taking shortcuts, you’ll likely find yourself stuck in a cycle of low-quality content that is difficult to overcome. Producing truly future proofing content takes time, effort, and commitment for the first time. However, in the long term, you will find it less troublesome. Students are also more likely to stick around to learn if the information they find is always relevant and useful.

### **To make the site easier to maintain**

1. Link to **ongoing webpages** instead of year-based links or documents. For example, link to the webpage where current handbooks are provided rather than linking to a handbook document for a specific year only.
2. Use relative dates instead of specific dates in site text or documents. For example, **Friday of Week 3** instead of 5th of October. (Submission deadlines in Ultra site can be automatically updated after rollover.)
3. Identify which materials may need to be manually updated for the next year. For example, **reading list links**, **embedded Panopto videos for specific cohorts**…etc.

!!! Warning
    Remember, even though the above strategies may be helpful, there is no fool-proof way to future-proof your content in Ultra site, and you will need to check your site is up to date after each rollover.
