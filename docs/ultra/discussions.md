To create a Discussion board in your Ultra site:

1. Hover over the grey divider where you want your discussion to appear, then click the plus icon.
2. Click **Create**. <br> ![Clicking the plus icon between two content items in the Course Content area and then clicking Create](images/discussions-plus-create.png)
3. Click **Discussion** under **Participation and Engagement**. <br>
![Clicking discussion under participation and engagement in the create item pane](images/discussions-create-discussion.png)
4. Click the discussion title and add a title for your Discussion board.
5. Click **What do you want to talk about?** and type instructions for the discussion. 
6. Click **Save**. <br> ![Clicking discussion under participation and engagement in the create item pane](images/discussions-title-topic.png)
7. Click the **Discussion Settings** gear icon.
8. If you want existing discussion posts to be invisible to students until they have posted, click **Post first**.
9. If you want to assign the discussion to particular groups of students, click **Assign to groups**.
10. Click **Save**. <br> ![Clicking discussion under participation and engagement in the create item pane](images/discussions-settings.png)
