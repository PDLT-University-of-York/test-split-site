# Guide title (short and to the point)

!!! Summary

    This guide provides step-by-step instructions on how to embed a Panopto video in to your Blackboard Ultra course. You will need to have Instructor (creator) access before starting.

### Text Steps

<!-- Clear and concise: Click **Submit**, not Click on the **Submit button** -->
<!-- Use **bold** to highight key tasks and features -->

1. **Hover** your mouse over the section of your ##Course Content## where you would like to embed your Panopto video. 
2. **Click** the purple '+' button, as shown below.
3. From the drop-down menu, **click** on the ##'Content Market'## button. 
4. **Click** on **Panopto Video** as shown below.
5. From the folder which appears, **search** or **select** the radio button beside the video you want to embed in to your site. 
6. From the bottom right of the screen, click the **'insert'** button.

!!! Tip

    NB: To embed videos which are ##not## linked to your site (such as in another module folder or in your My Folder), you will need to make a copy of the video and move it to your site folder so students can access the recordings.
