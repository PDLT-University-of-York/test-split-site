Navigating the Ultra system
The main site navigation bar appears on the left hand side on a larger screen, or can be accessed through the three lines icon in the top left on a smaller screen.

Navigation menu: described in text below

Use the menu to navigate between:

Your profile: manage your notification settings, avatar and personal information
Home: landing page with key system updates and departmental links
Activity: summary of activity collated across all your sites
Courses: access your module and other academic-related sites
Communities: access your non-academic sites, such as departmental noticeboards. If you are not enrolled on any Community sites, this option will not appear.
Calendar: collates Due Dates across all your sites. Does not integrate with the Timetable or other UoY systems.
Messages: summary of Messages (announcements for specific students) in your sites. These are not used much, and you may not see this item in your menu.
Marks: summary of assessments and marks received across your sites
Tools: access to some LTIs - it's not likely you'll need this page.
Your current page is highlighted in the menu.

Courses & Communities pages: access your sites
The Courses page lists all your module sites and other academic-related sites such as the Academic Integrity Tutorial and Turnitin Tutorial. The Communities page lists non-academic sites, like student noticeboard or departmental careers sites. Both pages work in the same way.

You can customise the pages in a number of ways:

Layout: select list or grid layout (grid layout contains image thumbnails)

Search courses by module/site name or keyword

Terms: select a specific academic year

Filters: various course types (which aren't used at UoY)

Items per page: Show 25, 50 or 100 sites per page

Favourite your modules: click the star icon to pin to the top of the default all sites list and any applicable search/filter results.



Navigating Ultra sites
General site navigation
Module site showing Content page. Menu above the banner, site content in the main section. Further described in text.
Use the navigation bar at the top to move between different parts of the site:

Content: the site homepage, where all your module content is found. This also shows the module teaching staff.
Calendar: Due Dates for assessments within the site. Note! This does not integrate with the Timetable or other UoY systems.
Announcements: updates from module staff.
Discussions: any discussions in the site, including any just for your specific group.
Gradebook: collates all assessments in the site.
Groups: details of any groups you are in for collaborative work.
You can also use this navigation bar to move between different sites or system areas:

Home: to close the site and return to the Courses page.
Courses: course switcher to move between recently-visited or favourite course sites.
Site sections
In most cases, sites should contain the following key sections:

Module information: an overview of the module, staff contact details, accessibility info and where to get help
Assessment: all the assessment information for the module
Reading List: containing all assigned reading for the module
Replay Lecture Capture (Panopto): the site's lecture capture recordings
Module materials: usually in weekly sections
Navigating content items
Most items should be organised within folders. Click a folder to show the content within it, and then click an item to open it.

Described in text

Once you open an item, a breadcrumb trail appears in the top bar to show the item's location in the site. Note that the folder links aren't clickable. Click the X button to close the item and return to your previous location.

For most folder types, there is a collapsible Contents panel to quickly jump to a specific item, and Previous / Next arrows in the top right to move between items. To help you focus, these disappear if you start an assessment attempt.
