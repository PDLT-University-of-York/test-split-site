Leave of Absence & marking    
Other:
- [Leave of Absence (LoA) and how it can impact submission and marking](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1gLyAEIMEfRzzzN2uSot_wKR9AkiHBVu-bxCY6gt5y68/edit#heading=h.kwjc6mnxsx6i).

Because students lose access to the VLE and Panopto when put on LoA in SITS if our team is not consulted, this can impact their ability to revise, prepare and submit assignments. It can also stop prior-made assignment submissions from being seen and/or marked by relevant staff members.
