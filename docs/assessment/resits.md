#  Resit specific considerations

!!! Summary

    This guide attempts to highlight considerations specific to handling resits when using TurnItIn, Ultra Assignment or Gradescope.

    It is primarily aimed at **administrators**.

When setting up resit submission points, there are some steps that will make this much simpler for students and also reduce the administrative burden:

1. Use a new submission point, and consider restricting this to only the students who need to resit.
2. Manage existing (non-resit) submission points to prevent resits being submitted to them.
3. There are some other general considerations that may be helpful, such as turning off *Automatic zeroes*.

For much more detail, see our full guide: [Assessment Administration - Resit specific considerations](https://docs.google.com/document/d/186ppjmnOD-8OXK23P7xy8FLbIl2TKvhFZ_0gTei0hqw/edit?usp=sharing)
