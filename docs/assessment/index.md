## Technical Limitations
When files (such as PDFs and DOCXs) are uploaded to the Learn VLE (vle.york.ac.uk) they are technically accessible to all students enrolled on the site that houses the files, even if adaptive/controlled release is in place. This is very important to know when planning to upload files critical to Assessments onto a VLE site.

Be sure to view and apply [our guidance on the safe upload and release of assessment-related files on the VLE](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1j6g1k2W0Ont1kA8DfSq7VuLYwgIhbDI7vzwd0-tQAaM/edit).
