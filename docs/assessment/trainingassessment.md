# Assessment Training

!!! Summary

    Lots of developments and changes are coming to Assessment in 2023/24. We will continually be updating our training offering for staff. See the pages linked below for details.


## Available Sessions
These sessions are running throughout August and September 2023.

- [Ultra Bitesize](https://vle-support.york.ac.uk/ultra/workshop-calendar/) - These sessions do 'deep dives' into different pieces of Ultra functionality, including
    - Creating and marking standard Ultra tests and submission points
    - Marking in TurnItIn Feedback Student
- [TurnItIn Assignment Admin Training](https://york-ac.csod.com/ui/lms-learning-details/app/event/ced6266b-61a8-40ef-ab63-5824fa2cdff7) - Specific training for admin' staff that need to manage anonymous, summative assignments for their department.
